{%- comment -%} The image viewer's dialog. assets/js/viewer.js fills and opens it, so a page that includes this loads that script (front matter: scripts: [viewer]). Its triggers are links or buttons with data-viewer="" and data-full (the image to show), and optionally data-title (the caption, which also names the dialog), data-desc, data-alt and data-paper (a page the viewer links to). With one trigger (a figure) the viewer shows no previous, next or count. The controls are inside the dialog, so a screen reader announces its name and keeps to it; the caption and count are a polite live region, so a new image is announced. Pass: id; label (read before the caption as the dialog's name, e.g. "Photo viewer"); noun (in the previous and next buttons' names, e.g. "photo"); paper=true for a "View the paper" link; notranslate=true when the captions are names (journal and year) that page translation must leave. {%- endcomment -%}
